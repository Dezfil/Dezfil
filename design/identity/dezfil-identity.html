Positioning

A rhumb-net for the whole product

A compass chart is the mariner's working chart — coastlines laced with rhumb lines radiating from compass roses, drawn to steer by bearing. It is the chart this identity is built on: instruments, harbors, and chart-making, one language end to end.

Not a full GIS. Not a data-warehouse frontend. A one-container, file-native, database-capable spatial SQL builder for publishing maps cheaply.

Surfaces

Nine roles, one set of values

A surface is named for what it is to a person, and its role holds how see-through it is, its blur, its edge, its corner and its shadow. A surface reads its role and never types one of those values, so both themes, a published page and an exported image take them from one place, and a look that restates a role moves every surface of it. Corners stay on the radius family, so the Corners pick still reaches them. Each sample below is drawn by its role's own tokens over a hatched ground; the values, the steps and the rows are fed from the shipped tokens.

Outside the roles, each by name: the side rail opened over the page (solid, the lift and its one side shadow), the dialog's veil and the mini-map's, the wash under a small map while it loads and on the URL tray, the image's own credit, title line and failure note, the search marker's pin, and the tints and fades that are not surfaces. A primary button that carries a shadow is a button: it takes the float step and no role.

Motion

Four motion roles, one instrument

The panel move is the hero: every panel, dock, and card animates on one easing and duration, and the map card goes true full-bleed and back with everything still floating above it. Three supporting roles complete the vocabulary: micro state flips at 0.13s, entrances at 0.22s on the settle curve, and transient feedback — save whispers, copied-confirmations — fading on a 0.35s beat after a readable hold. The workspace settle-in composes the entrance curve on a longer beat. Three continuous identities are recorded exceptions rather than roles, unchanged in code: the compass's linear tracking, the indefinite tile-preparation pulse, and the drag settle-and-reveal on its own 250ms curve. All motion collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

Rulebook

The rules, ratified

The identity's habits, ratified as rules. Surfaces adopt them as they are touched, and the repository's own checks hold them once adopted.

R1The type ramp, floor 11pxSix roles (the ladder above); nothing renders below 11px, and in-between sizes snap to the nearest rung as surfaces are touched. Weights are 400/500/600 only.
R2The mono boundaryMono carries code and data — SQL, dataset-table and dataset-list values, schema and machine identifiers, expression inputs, feature readouts, raw errors — never control labels, buttons, prose sentences, or chrome figures: counts and coordinates read the UI face, tabular where steadiness matters.
R3The ink hierarchyFull ink for anything you act on or need to read — names, values, labels of controls. Muted only for secondary description. Faint only for decoration-grade text — never a value, never a label, never anything you'd miss if it vanished. Each application is checked against rendered evidence; where the rule worsens a surface, that returns as a question, never a silent exception.
R4Drawn icons, three tiersSVG icons only — action at 13px/2.0 stroke, chrome normally at 16px with a 1.6–2.0 stroke, illustrative at 20px/1.5. Chrome may use a 22px filled directional mark where that geometry needs the control height; the stage compass is the named instance. Unicode glyphs (▸ ✓ ＋ ◇ ⚠ …) are banned in UI chrome.
R5The radius family, closedsm 4 · 9 · control 12 · lg 14 · card 20 · rail 18 · pill. Everything snaps to family and reads it through its token, so the Corners pick reaches every rounded edge; only pills and rounds are written as values.
R6Four motion rolesPanel 0.3s, micro 0.13s, entrance 0.22s on the settle curve, feedback 0.35s after a readable hold — all collapsing under reduced motion. Three continuous identities are named exceptions, not a fifth role, and stay as they are in code: the compass's linear tracking, the indefinite tile-preparation pulse, and the drag settle-and-reveal on its 250ms bespoke curve.
R7Control tiers and the hit-area contractThree tiers — 38px chrome (carrying R4's chrome icon), 30px compact, and icon-action, whose visual size follows the icon tiers (R4) while its hit target is the contract's floor. The contract: every interactive target offers at least 24×24px of hit area regardless of visual size.
R8One primary: flat accentThe flat accent button is the only primary treatment; the gradient is retired.
R9One field focusA focused field tints its border to the accent, and the global :focus-visible ring is never suppressed.
R10The voice, in five lines1 · Plain sentences in the UI face — never mono for prose. 2 · Say what happened, then what to do next. 3 · Use the user's words — the vocabulary users bring from other map tools, no spec or engine terms. 4 · Errors are dismissible, human first, engine detail behind a disclosure. 5 · One idea per sentence; no exclamation marks.

Components

The specimen sheet

The primitive kit, rendered by its own stylesheets. Every src/components/ui/*.module.css is fed into this page by scripts/build-specimen.mjs with its selectors namespaced and its declarations untouched, so the buttons, fields, slider, tabs, dialog, menu trigger, icon actions, failure note, async states and status bars below are wearing the shipped rules — not a copy of them. The markup and the captions are authored, and the surfaces with no primitive behind them — chips, the layer card, the legend row, the choice segments — say so.

Hover and tab through them: every active state is the same kelp treatment (color, a soft --accent-dim wash, an inset ring), and every focus lands the one global ring.

Button — Button.module.css · one primary, flat accent (R8)
38px chrome tier
30px compact tier

Four variants, two tiers, and that is the whole set, the gradient primary retired. Busy and disabled both read without color: the label stays, a spinner joins it, and the cursor follows the app-wide convention.

Field — Field.module.css

One focus treatment (R9): the border tints to the accent and the global ring is never suppressed. The checkbox is a real input with a drawn box over it, so keyboard and form behavior are the browser's — and its hit area reaches 24px outward without moving a neighbour.

Switch — Switch.module.css

The word for one immediate on/off state. A real checkbox with the switch role under a drawn track and thumb — Space, focus and screen readers are the platform's. Checkboxes stay the word for picking many from a list; two sizes is how a vocabulary stops being one, so there is exactly this size.

Slider — Slider.module.css

A native range input, skinned: a 4px track that fills to the value, a 15px knob that grows on hover. Deliberately not a component — every caller drives the fill through its own custom property and keeps the range semantics the keyboard already gets for free.

IconButton — IconButton.module.css

One action string is both the accessible name and the visible hint, so the two cannot drift — hover or tab to a mark to see it. The icon stays on the 13px action tier while the button's own box reaches the 24×24px hit floor (R7); a bigger target never means bigger artwork.

Menu — Menu.module.css

The primitive styles one thing — the trigger's 24px hit floor, which is not an adopter's to opt out of — and owns everything else: haspopup and expanded on the trigger, focus into the menu on a keyboard opening, arrows and Home/End, Escape returning focus to the trigger, an outside click closing without stealing focus. The sheet's look is the surface's, shown open here.

A plain sentence in the UI face, saying what happened and what to do next; the engine's words are collapsed behind a disclosure, closed by default. Prose set in mono reads as a stack trace, which is exactly what made the app's failure moments feel debug-grade.

AsyncState — AsyncState.module.css

Loading layers…

No layers yet. Add one to start the map.

Four mutually exclusive states — pending, empty, failed, content — and empty is only ever shown after a request resolved. A screen that starts its collection at zero and renders empty immediately tells you there is nothing there while the request is still in flight. Failure is the note above; retry re-requests, and a late answer from a superseded attempt can never overwrite a fresh one.

Principles

What the identity stands on

One language — the naming pool ships from ships, harbors & chart-making

The implementation is the source of truth — and half this page is generated from it. Three regions are machine-fed by scripts/build-specimen.mjs and marked as such in the source: the light, dark and OS-preference token blocks, from src/app/globals.css; the primitive kit's component CSS, from every src/components/ui/*.module.css, selectors namespaced and declarations untouched; and the palette ramps, from src/lib/style/palettes.ts. Regeneration must reproduce the committed page byte for byte — npm run lint:specimen fails if any of the three has drifted from its source, so they cannot go stale.

Everything else is authored: the demonstrations, the shell diagram, the ramp and chart rendering, and all of the prose. Where a demonstration has a kit primitive behind it, it is built from the fed kit classes rather than a lookalike; the rest — chips, the layer card, the legend row, the segmented and view toggles — are the app's own surfaces, drawn here on the fed tokens. This authored half was last checked against the shipped kit on 2026-08-08 and carries no automatic guarantee between refreshes. The typefaces are the real embedded faces and the texture is the real shell sheet.
